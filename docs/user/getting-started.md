# Getting started

Jarling is a local-first envelope budgeting application. It helps you decide
what the money you already own should do next instead of only explaining where
it went afterward.

## The basic cycle

1. Create accounts using their real current balances.
2. Give available money jobs by assigning it to categories.
3. Record transactions and keep their cleared state aligned with your bank.
4. Move money when priorities change.
5. Reconcile accounts periodically.

The four main areas are **Budget**, **Accounts**, **Transactions**, and
**Reports**. Jarling stores one plan on the device and does not require a
Jarling account or internet connection for its core features.

## Create the first account

Choose an account type that matches how the money behaves:

- Cash accounts such as checking, savings, and cash hold money you own.
- Credit cards and lines of credit hold borrowed money that must be repaid.
- Tracking accounts and loans contribute to Net Worth but remain outside the
  budget.

An opening balance establishes the starting ledger balance. For an on-budget
cash account, owned money becomes available in **Ready to Assign**. Credit debt
does not create budgetable money.

## Build the first budget

Open Budget, select the month, and assign Ready to Assign to categories such as
rent, groceries, or transport. Assigning is not spending: it places existing
money in an envelope. A transaction later changes the category's Activity and
Available balance.

Start with the bills due before the next expected income, then fund everyday
needs and longer-term targets. The plan can be changed at any time with Move
Money; a useful budget is expected to adapt.
